How this works / troubleshooting

Point your camera at a barcode. When it's recognized, it's added to the list below with a timestamp and beep/vibration confirmation. Duplicate codes are still added but highlighted in orange so you can spot repeats.

Your list is saved on this device only (browser local storage) so it survives an accidental refresh, until you tap "Clear All".

Camera not starting? Browsers only allow camera access on secure pages (HTTPS) or when opened from localhost. If the camera won't start, use the manual entry field instead, or open this file from a properly HTTPS-hosted location.
